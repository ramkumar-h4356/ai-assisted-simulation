## Interpretation boundary

A passing M26 report establishes consistency of the checked artifact, execution trace and user-declared checks. It does not establish empirical accuracy, causal validity, scientific truth, formal correctness, or generalization to real-world systems. Empirical validation requires independent observations, suitable statistical methods and domain review.
